Back up everything (one file)

Backs up only your apps' data (the data-storing apps above) into a single file — one LocalSend transfer to your other device instead of five. Import writes that data back.
Heads-up: this works in a normal browser tab (best on your PC, where all apps share storage). On iPhone, apps you added to the Home Screen as separate icons may keep separate storage — there, each app's own Export/Import is the reliable path. Importing here overwrites the listed apps' data on this device.
